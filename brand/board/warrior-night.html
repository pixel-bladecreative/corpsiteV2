Mechanics

Modern meets ancient.

  • No hat, hood up

    The hat belongs to the other two — this is the distinguishing silhouette. Face in shadow beneath the hood, jaw may catch light. Not the Samurai's total blackout.

  • Reads young

    Lighter build, casual weight, unhurried posture.

  • Modified streetwear

    Oversized hood, loose cargo, sealed seams, magnetic closures, hard-shell panels at shoulder and forearm over matte black articulated plating.

  • No drawn weapon

    A cybernetic arm instead. He has not needed one across three plates and it is stronger that way — his hands are the weapon.

  • Desaturated Tokyo

    Signage dim and near-grey, never a neon blaze. No magenta, no hot pink, no orange. The city stays dark so he can be the only lit thing.

  • The common thread

    Hem, sleeve, lower legs stop being solid and become wet black ink and smoky shadow. Present in every plate, non-negotiable.
