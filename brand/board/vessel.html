Pixel Blade · the mark · round six

The
Vessel

Five rounds died trying to draw a logo. This one stops trying. A ring with real thickness, stood upright, cupping a world that was built rather than found — and the Monk sitting in it.

Day mode first, as asked. Night follows, and it is not only a matter of turning the light down.

01 · What the references actually share

One grammar,
stated five times

Not five references — one set of mechanics repeated. These are the parts that transfer. Palette does not; structure does.

  1. 01

    The circle is never a stroke

    Not once, in any of the five. A solid vessel, an annulus with weather in its rim, a flat disc holding a world, a light brighter than the page. This independently confirms the finding that killed four rounds: outlines were the mistake.

  2. 02

    The circle is a container, or a hole

    The world is always on one side of it — inside the cup, inside the rim, or absent altogether. This is the aperture, which the handoff filed as homeless. Four of the five say it is not homeless.

  3. 03

    Chroma is one event, particulate, low coverage

    Red at one clock position. Red only in falling debris. Blossom scatter. A single red seed. Never a fill, never a field. Every reference is already far under the 25% ceiling and the colour still lands, because it is the only one.

  4. 04

    Material leaves downward, and coarsens

    Debris, blossom, suspended seeds. Never upward, never inward. The Ninja's dissolve rule, arrived at independently.

  5. 05

    Hairlines hang; they never outline

    Long vertical threads descending from the subject, terminating in a small specimen. A measuring line, not a contour line — which is why it does not violate the no-stroke finding.

  6. 06

    Pale ground, half the frame empty

    Fifty to seventy percent of every reference is nothing at all. All five are day mode.

  7. 07

    The figure is low, silhouetted, and does not interact

    Seated or walking, at the foot of the impossible object, never reaching for it.

02 · The die

Geometry,
struck in code

Two discs, one subtracted. The body is given a real cross-section — a rounded shoulder with a concave scoop cut near the inner edge — and lit by the house light at 214°, z 0.34. The scoop is the whole argument: its far wall turns back into the light and becomes the pale band that says vessel rather than ring.

Nothing here is drawn. There is no outline anywhere in the file — every edge is a place where two values meet. This is the deliverable the four rejected rounds were reaching for and never built.

03 · The impression

Four readings,
day mode

The die proves the object. It cannot render a garden — code-drawn vegetation reads as clip art, and pretending otherwise would repeat round four's mistake of re-rendering something instead of rethinking it. So the world is generated and the geometry is not.

All four pass check-plate.py --day: chroma coverage 0.1–1.4% against a 25% ceiling, light ground 73–75%, edge delta 1. Every one feathered to #F4F5F2.

04 · The switch

Night is not
a dimmer

Your guess was that night might just be turning the light down. Close, but the object found the real mechanism on its own: the inner scoop goes pale grey in day and bronze at night.

That is precisely what tokens.css already says — bronze measures 2.05:1 on paper and cannot cross, so on the light ground its role passes to oxide. The crescent obeys the rule without being told: rust pine and grey scoop by day, ember pine and bronze scoop by night. Same geometry, same light position, roles held, values swapped.

05 · What it costs

It dies small

You said cohesion and scale trump the guidelines, and the mark gets its exception. It still has a floor. At 256px everything reads. At 128 the crescent and the tree hold and the Monk becomes a dot. At 64 it is a crescent with something in it. At 32 it is a crescent.

This is not a defect to fix — it is the price of the reframe, and it is worth paying. But it means the system needs a companion form for favicon and app-icon sizes, and that form is almost certainly the bare crescent silhouette with the scoop. Worth deciding before phase 3.

07 · Open

Three decisions

Which reading

C is the strongest — the tree leaving through the mouth breaks the disc, which is the move struck said to borrow. A is the most faithful and the safest. D introduces the mirror floor from dreamed garden without being asked to.

The Monk's hat

monk.md gives him no hat — that is the Samurai's. The kasa here is a legibility decision, not a lore one, and at this scale it is the only thing that survives the reduction. If you keep it, monk.md needs a line saying the hat is permitted at mark scale, or the next session will read it as a contradiction.

The small form

Nothing below about 96px carries the world. Either the bare crescent becomes the reduction, or the mark simply does not go small and the favicon is something else entirely.
