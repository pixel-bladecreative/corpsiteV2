The crane

Retire it as the mark. Keep it as a motif.

Across two rounds every crane attempt landed in the same three ditches: fussy as a wireframe, generic as faceted origami, or off-thesis as a kamon. That is not bad luck three times — a crane is a picture of a thing, and this brand's whole argument is reduction.

Meanwhile the circle turned up unprompted three separate times: the garden's backlit disc, the Monk's brush ring, the Samurai's enso. Nobody asked for it.

Mark and motif are different jobs. The bronze crane stays — in the garden, in imagery, as an object in the world. It just stops being the thing at 16 pixels in a browser tab.

Exploration

Six directions, generated.

Free reign, six theories of what the mark is. Useful for finding the ideas — and useless as marks, which is the point made below.

Generation gives concepts. It does not give marks.

Every one of these is a logo-image — shaded, illustrative, unrepeatable, wrong at small size. Same lesson as the pixel dissolution: the ideas came from generating, the artefacts had to be built. Everything below is real geometry in brand/make-marks.py, output as SVG.

The recommendation

Not a choice. A system.

01 is the mark. 02 is the mark dissolving.

01 rests. Favicon, nav, signature, print, anywhere small or permanent. It survives every size in one colour on both grounds.

02 moves. Hero, large format, loading, hover, scroll. Same circle, losing resolution — which means the logo animates using the same algorithm as the Ninja, and the site already has that code.

A mark with a resting state and a dissolving state is not a compromise between two options. It is the brand's central idea — things come apart at the edges — stated by the logo itself.

Lockup · both modes

Mark, wordmark, descriptor. The wordmark's tracking is deliberately wide enough that the mark's counter and the letter-spacing read at the same rhythm.

Why this one

What the circle is carrying.

  • It showed up alone

    Three times unprompted across the garden, the Monk and the Samurai. The system chose it before I did.

  • Ancient form, modern act

    An enso is a brush gesture a thousand years old. The cut through it is precise, square-ended and instrumental. That is the whole company in one shape.

  • It is not a picture of anything

    Which means it cannot date, and it cannot be read as a bird, a swoosh or a badge.

  • Reduction, obeyed

    The brand's argument is cutting back until one true thing is left. A mark that is a circle and one cut has taken its own advice.

  • It holds the others

    The garden's disc, the Monk's ring — the mark and the world are already the same shape.

  • Both modes, one colour

    No adaptation, no alternate version, no reversed artwork. Bronze on sumi, oxide on paper.

Open: the exact cut angle and slip distance, whether the descriptor stays "Advertising", and the wordmark typeface — which is the same unargued proposal flagged at the top of this page.
