The crane

Retire it as the mark. Keep it as a motif.

Across two rounds every crane attempt landed in the same three ditches: fussy as a wireframe, generic as faceted origami, or off-thesis as a kamon. That is not bad luck three times — a crane is a picture of a thing, and this brand's whole argument is reduction.

Meanwhile the circle turned up unprompted three separate times: the garden's backlit disc, the Monk's brush ring, the Samurai's enso. Nobody asked for it.

Mark and motif are different jobs. The bronze crane stays — in the garden, in imagery, as an object in the world. It just stops being the thing at 16 pixels in a browser tab.

Exploration

Free reign, six theories of what the mark is. Useful for finding the ideas — and useless as marks, which is the point made below.

Generation gives concepts. It does not give marks.

Every one of these is a logo-image — shaded, illustrative, unrepeatable, wrong at small size. Same lesson as the pixel dissolution: the ideas came from generating, the artefacts had to be built. Everything below is real geometry in brand/make-marks.py, output as SVG.

The recommendation

Not a choice. A system.

01 is the mark. 02 is the mark dissolving.

01 rests. Favicon, nav, signature, print, anywhere small or permanent. It survives every size in one colour on both grounds.

02 moves. Hero, large format, loading, hover, scroll. Same circle, losing resolution — which means the logo animates using the same algorithm as the Ninja, and the site already has that code.

A mark with a resting state and a dissolving state is not a compromise between two options. It is the brand's central idea — things come apart at the edges — stated by the logo itself.

Lockup · both modes

Mark, wordmark, descriptor. The wordmark's tracking is deliberately wide enough that the mark's counter and the letter-spacing read at the same rhythm.

Why this one

What the circle is carrying.

  • It showed up alone

    Three times unprompted across the garden, the Monk and the Samurai. The system chose it before I did.

  • Ancient form, modern act

    An enso is a brush gesture a thousand years old. The cut through it is precise, square-ended and instrumental. That is the whole company in one shape.

  • It is not a picture of anything

    Which means it cannot date, and it cannot be read as a bird, a swoosh or a badge.

  • Reduction, obeyed

    The brand's argument is cutting back until one true thing is left. A mark that is a circle and one cut has taken its own advice.

  • It holds the others

    The garden's disc, the Monk's ring — the mark and the world are already the same shape.

  • Both modes, one colour

    No adaptation, no alternate version, no reversed artwork. Bronze on sumi, oxide on paper.

Open: the exact cut angle and slip distance, whether the descriptor stays "Advertising", and the wordmark typeface — which is the same unargued proposal flagged at the top of this page.

Act four · the crane, returned

It does not sit inside the ring. It crosses it.

A bird placed politely in a circle is a badge, and badges are fussy. The move that makes this work is occlusion: the ring is genuinely absent where the crane passes in front of it, masked out rather than painted over. The bird is then defined as much by what it interrupts as by what it draws — which is exactly what lets it stay this reduced.

What the geometry is doing

Six decisions, each one earned by a failure.

  • One wing, not two

    Symmetrical wings read as a butterfly, and inside a circle they go heraldic immediately. One wing plus two opposing spikes reads as a crane, and it can bank.

  • The neck is a line with a kink

    Drawn as a filled taper it reads as a spear. The bend near the tip is the head, and it is the difference between a bird and a dart. It is the smallest element here and it does the most work.

  • One crease, in the page ground

    A single line splitting the wing into two planes. A flat shape with a fold in it reads as paper; without it the wing reads as a feather. That one line is the entire origami cue.

  • The mask dilation is clearance

    The ring stops short of the bird rather than touching it. Without that gap the two shapes fuse into one blob three steps down the scale ladder.

  • The bank

    Ten degrees. Enough that the mark has a direction of travel, not enough that it looks tipped over.

  • The cut moved

    To the lower left — the one quadrant the bird leaves alone. Two events in the same place is noise; two events on opposite sides is composition.

Scale

The honest answer: this one does not go to sixteen.

At 64 it is a bird in a circle. At 32 it is a bird in a circle if you know to look. At 16 it is a smudge — and no amount of tuning fixes that, because a crane needs four shapes and sixteen pixels will hold two.

Where this leaves it

Open, and needing your eye.

  • Does it read as origami?

    It reads as a folded bird. Whether it reads as specifically a paper crane is the call I cannot make for you — I have been staring at it too long. If it lands as "angular bird" rather than "crane", say so and I will take another run at the head and the keel.

  • Direction of travel

    It banks with the wing to the upper left. Mirrored is one number, and I can show you both side by side.

  • How far the bird overruns

    The wing tip and head currently break the ring by a few pixels. It can be pulled fully inside, or pushed much further out so the bird is passing through the ring rather than held by it.

  • 05 or 06

    Whole ring, or the ring with your cut in it. 06 carries more of the brand's argument; 05 is calmer and more of a seal.
