The instruction that works is not "dissolve him"

It is: the figure is only partially drawn.

  • Compose the shadow first. The frame needs a large unbroken field of near-black running off an edge. Without somewhere to dissolve into, the effect cannot exist — and the generator defaults to floating ink.
  • Name what is drawn. "Left side, shoulder to hip, crisp and solid."
  • Name what is not. "Right side and both legs — do not draw them. No silhouette, no outline, no hint of a limb."
  • Make the transition wide. A third of the body, values stepping down, folds and panel lines dropping out as they go.
  • Ban the objects — tendrils, wisps, plumes, tips, ends. Every time.

The figure's black and the field's black are the same black, one continuous area.

A trap worth knowing

"Ragged" summons wisps.

Asking for a torn or irregular boundary reliably reintroduces exactly what the rule forbids. The generator's default reading of "irregular dissolve" is tendrils.

Recorded in the namespace: always pair a raggedness instruction with the explicit ban.

Adaptive colour

He is the day/night switch.

Light enough to vanish into a crowd, dark when he needs to be. Measured across the same garment, the same silhouette swings from value ~76% to ~19%.

Light · blending

Standing at the mouth of the alley — the only shadow available on a bright street, and he is already half into it.

Dark · working

Same garment, same cut. Signage kept near-grey so he is the only lit thing.

Useful beyond the fiction

He does in one figure what the site does across pages — the natural transition device between the light pages and the dark ones. The only element that belongs to both modes at once and changes by choice rather than by placement.

Mechanics

Modern meets ancient.

  • No hat, hood up

    The hat belongs to the Monk and the Samurai. Inside the hood: a complete void — no skin, no jaw, no eyes.

  • Layered and long

    Parka hem well below the hip toward the knee, worn open over an offset mid-layer, a high-collared under-layer and stacked cargo. Each hem at a different length. Never cropped — the bulk is the silhouette.

  • Reads young

    Lighter build, casual weight, unhurried posture.

  • No drawn weapon

    A cybernetic arm instead. He has not needed one across six plates — his hands are the weapon.

  • Desaturated Tokyo

    Signage dim and near-grey, never a neon blaze. The city stays dark so he can be the only lit thing.

  • Kindle, hairline

    Narrow cyan channels at 0.4–1.0% of frame coverage. --pb-kindle marks what is not proven yet — and a myth qualifies.
