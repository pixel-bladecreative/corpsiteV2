A full-figure martial arts poster on a plain warm paper-white ground, with generous empty space around the figure.

THE FIGURE: a lean shaven-headed monk, a fictional character, caught mid-turn in a slow sweeping movement — one arm extended and reaching, the other drawn low across the body, weight on a bent front leg. The pose reads as a dance as much as a strike. His head is tilted down and his eyes are closed. His face is completely calm — no aggression, no strain, no shout. He carries NO WEAPON; both hands are empty and open.

THE WARDROBE — the most important part, many distinct layers of cloth, each a different weave and a different value, all worn and honestly aged:
  - an under-robe of fine bone-white linen, soft and close
  - over it a heavy coarse hemp robe in warm grey-tan, stiff and sculptural, its hem torn into long ragged tongues that fan out with the movement
  - a broad rope sash wound many times around the waist in dark rope-brown
  - forearms and shins bound in wrapped strips of frayed cloth and cord
  - a loose ochre-tan panel hanging from one shoulder
Each layer catches light differently — linen soft, hemp coarse and fibrous, rope dry and hard. Real cloth, real weight, real fraying.

DISSOLUTION: the outer edges of the cloth — the torn hem, the trailing sleeve, the far shoulder — break apart into fine drifting particles, dust and pale mist, and dissolve into the empty paper. He is not fully contained by his own outline. He is sharp and in focus where he is solid, never motion-blurred.

MOTION AS BRUSHWORK: sweeping calligraphic ink ribbons spiral around and past him, drawn as real wet brush strokes with visible bristle texture and tapered ends. They are in charcoal grey-black, with a few in deep teal and a few in burnt rust orange. They describe the arc his limbs have travelled. Fine spatter and grit follows them.

Behind: faint ghosted grey brush marks on the paper, very pale, almost invisible.

Color: warm paper white, bone, warm grey-tan, rope brown, ochre, with charcoal, deep teal and burnt rust in the brushwork only. Restrained.

Style: dreamlike, elegant, weightless, precise. Watercolour, mist and motion. NOT a photograph of a real person, NOT a film still, no modern clothing, no logos.

No text, no letters, no calligraphy characters, no seals, no watermark.
