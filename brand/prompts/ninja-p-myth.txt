Anime key art, Shinji Aramaki style — hard-surface design, crisp panel lines, cel-shaded flat colour.

SETTING: the aftermath. A dim future-Tokyo service corridor of grey machine panelling, dead screens and cable runs. Fine dust in the air, one weak cold overhead light. The corridor floor and the far end fall into deep near-black. Something has already happened here and it is over. Roughly seventy-five percent of the frame is near-black.

THE FIGURE stands in the middle distance facing away, heavily layered — a LONG oversized dark hooded parka with the hem well below the hip toward the knee, over an offset mid-layer, a high-collared under-layer, stacked cargo trousers, hard-shell panels over matte black plating. Hood up, head slightly lowered, arms at his sides, one matte black cybernetic hand with narrow cyan-turquoise hairline channels. THE FACE IS A COMPLETE VOID. Utterly still.

THE EFFECT — HIS COAT IS BEING EATEN AWAY INTO PIXELS. This happens ON THE GARMENT ITSELF, not in the air beside him:

The left edge and bottom hem of his parka IS NOT A SMOOTH OUTLINE. It has been CHEWED into a jagged STAIRCASE of missing square bites — the cloth is visibly narrower on that side and along the hem than it should be, with square notches cut into the sleeve, the shoulder, the lapel and the hem. You can see holes: single square gaps punched through the cloth a little way in from the edge, showing the background through them. The garment is visibly incomplete.

The blocks are LARGE and unmistakably SQUARE, like a 16-bit video game sprite — hard right angles, flat solid colour, no anti-aliasing.

Immediately outside that chewed edge, the loose squares CONTINUE — and there must be NO GAP between the cloth and them. They begin exactly where the cloth ends, as though they have only just come free of it. From there they DRIFT UPWARD AND OUTWARD, as if lifted on a breeze rising from below: dense and tightly packed right against the coat, spreading and thinning as they rise, the furthest few sparse and scattered.

EVERY LOOSE SQUARE IS THE COLOUR OF THE CLOTH IT CAME FROM — bone white, pale sand, grey, charcoal, black. They are pieces of his coat. They are NOT cyan, NOT blue, NOT the colour of the wall, and they do NOT glow. Only one or two of the hundreds may carry the cyan of the plating beneath.

His right side, catching the overhead light, is completely intact — smooth cloth, clean unbroken anime outline.

FORBIDDEN: smoke, mist, ink, tendrils, wisps, ribbons, dust clouds, embers, sparks. FORBIDDEN: glitch art, RGB channel splitting, scan lines, chromatic aberration, digital noise, static, holograms, glowing particles. Plain flat opaque squares of cloth colour.

No bodies, no gore, no visible damage — only emptiness.
Color: near-black, cold machine grey, faint steel, narrow cyan-turquoise.
Style: cel-shaded anime. Quiet, cold, ominous. NOT photorealistic.
No text, no readable letters, no watermark, NO FACE, no blood, no bodies.
