A single sculptural object photographed in a quiet studio against a warm off-white paper background. The object is a large upright crescent: a thick disc of matte cast concrete with a second disc subtracted from it, leaving an open crescent standing on a pale surface. The concrete is fine-grained, matte and non-reflective, dark charcoal grey, with visible surface tooth. Its concave inner face is scooped and catches the light as a pale grey band; the outer shoulder rolls away into deep shadow. One narrow near-white specular line runs along the lit rim. A single raking light, low, from the upper left. Short soft contact shadow beneath the object.

Cradled in the crescent's cup sits a small staged landscape: a low dark rock outcrop, and a windswept pine with flat horizontal pads of foliage in muted rust and burnt orange, its trunk sweeping left and reaching out through the crescent's opening. A tiny seated figure in a broad flat conical straw hat rests on the ledge at the left, in silhouette, head lowered, hands empty, occupying no more than a twentieth of the frame height.

Muted, restrained, physical. Vast empty space around the object; more than half the frame is plain pale ground. Colour appears only in the pine. No text, no lettering, no signage, no cherry blossom, no torii gate, no calligraphy, no seal stamps, no distant mountain. No glossy bevel, no drop shadow, no outline, no gradient standing in for a highlight.
