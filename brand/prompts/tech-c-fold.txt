A structure FLOATING in a vast empty field of warm paper-white, with no table, no ground and no room — enormous emptiness on all sides.

THE SUBJECT: a building that is unmistakably FOLDED as well as built. Strict ninety-degree architectural planes in the spirit of Tadao Ando meet crisp folded paper geometry — long horizontal cantilevered slabs, and beside and beneath them, planes of heavy matte paper creased into sharp angular facets, as though the same structure had been partly built and partly folded from a single sheet.

MATERIALS, honestly textured: fine matte grey concrete card, warm wood veneer with visible grain, hairline brushed steel edges, and heavy matte CONSTRUCTION PAPER in burnt rust and deep teal — creased, cut clean, slightly fibrous at the edges, each fold catching light on one face and falling to shadow on the other.

Warm amber light glows from deep inside the folded mass. It is the only warm light.

THE DRAFTING LAYER: hairline charcoal and rust-red lines run along every crease and continue FAR out past the object into the empty white, dissolving completely to nothing before the frame edges. A few pale grey soft-edged circles drift behind, like clouds through paper.

Color: warm paper white, matte charcoal, warm wood tan, deep teal, burnt rust, one amber glow.

Style: dreamlike, weightless, serene, achingly beautiful. Where engineering and folding become the same act. NOT cyberpunk, NOT neon, NOT sci-fi, no holograms, no people, no tabletop.

No text, no readable letters, no watermark, no logo.
