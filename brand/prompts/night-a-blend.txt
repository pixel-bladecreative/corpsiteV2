Anime key art in the style of Shinji Aramaki — hard-surface mechanical design, crisp panel lines, cel-shaded flat colour zones with sharp specular edges. Precise and engineered, not painterly.

THE FIGURE: a young cyber-ninja standing motionless in a daytime crowd on a future Tokyo street, three-quarter on. He is dressed to disappear, and he is HEAVILY LAYERED:
  - a LONG oversized bone-white hooded parka, hem falling well BELOW THE HIP toward the knee, worn open, heavy fabric with real weight and deep folds
  - beneath it a second pale sand mid-layer jacket, also long, offset from the first
  - beneath that a high-collared pale grey under-shirt
  - loose pale cargo trousers, stacked and bunched at the ankle
  - modified streetwear detailing: sealed seams, magnetic closures, webbing straps, small hard-shell panels at shoulder and forearm
Layers upon layers, each hem visible at a different length. Nothing cropped short.

THE FACE IS A COMPLETE VOID. The hood is up and pulled forward and inside it there is NOTHING — pure flat black. No skin, no jaw, no chin, no mouth, no eyes, no features of any kind. Only the shape of the hood and darkness. This is absolute.

TECH: one matte black cybernetic hand with articulated finger joints hanging relaxed. Narrow cyan-turquoise light in hairline channels along the underlayer — a few millimetres wide, never a glow-suit.

THE DISSOLUTION: an ENTIRE REGION of him is simply not there. His whole lower body from mid-thigh down, and the entire trailing half of the parka, have stopped being cloth and become churning WET BLACK INK — a large mass of curling tendrils, torn ribbons and drifting droplets. This is not smoke from a point and not a wisp; it is a whole part of him missing and replaced by ink.

SETTING: a bright overcast future Tokyo street — pale concrete, glass, tall signage, the crowd rendered soft and out of focus, moving past him while he stands still.

Color: bone white, pale sand, cool concrete grey, matte black, and narrow cyan-turquoise light. No neon, no magenta, no orange.

Style: Shinji Aramaki anime, cel-shaded, hard-surface. NOT photorealistic, NOT 3D render.

No text, no letters, no watermark, no logo, NO FACE, no skin visible anywhere above the neck.
