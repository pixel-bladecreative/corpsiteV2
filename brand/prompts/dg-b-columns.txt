A vast dark interior, photographed straight on. A constructed installation, not a real room.

Standing free on a seamless black mirror floor: a series of ENORMOUS luminous vertical columns, each many storeys tall, at varying widths and at clearly different depths. They are lit from within and glow soft warm bronze, with fine vertical striations running their length. Their vertical edges catch as razor-thin near-white lines. They have no visible fixings and no ceiling above them.

Beyond and between the columns, a landscape rendered as FLAT GRAPHIC PLANES rather than as a photograph: layered mountain silhouettes in deep blue-green teal, each successive layer paler, hazier and lower in contrast than the one in front, stacked like cut paper into the far distance. No photographic texture — these are flat cut shapes.

A single human figure walks between the columns, seen from behind, rendered TINY at roughly three percent of the frame height.

The mirror floor doubles the columns into the lower third as long soft warm reflections.

Light: the columns are the only light source. Deep black between them, and roughly seventy percent of the frame carries no detail.

Color: deep void black, warm bronze glowing columns, deep blue-green teal mountain planes, one near-white edge line. Absolutely nothing else — no coral, no red, no pink, no cherry blossom, no purple.

Style: constructed, staged, calm and strange. Silent. High-end exhibition photography.

No text, no letters, no calligraphy, no watermark, no logo.
