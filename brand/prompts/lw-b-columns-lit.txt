A vast dark interior, photographed straight on. A constructed installation, not a real room.

THE SPACE: a seamless black mirror floor and total void above. Standing free on it, enormous luminous vertical columns, many storeys tall, at varying widths and clearly different depths. The columns glow warm bronze with fine vertical striations, their edges catching razor-thin near-white lines. No fixings, no ceiling.

BEYOND THE COLUMNS — a landscape rendered as FLAT GRAPHIC PLANES, and unlike the room it is richly saturated: ranks of coral, vermilion and burnt-orange ridges in the middle distance, warm rose mist between them, deepening into blue-green teal mountains at the back, each successive layer paler and hazier. Cut-paper shapes, no photographic texture. It glows like a lit transparency.

One human figure walks between the columns, seen from behind, TINY at roughly three percent of the frame height, in full dark silhouette.

The mirror floor doubles the columns and picks up only the faintest warm reflection of the distant landscape.

CRITICAL: the saturated color exists ONLY in the far landscape. The columns, the floor, the figure and the void stay strictly bronze, black and teal. The warm color must not bloom or spill into the room.

Style: constructed, silent, calm and strange. High-end exhibition photography.

No text, no letters, no calligraphy, no watermark, no logo.
