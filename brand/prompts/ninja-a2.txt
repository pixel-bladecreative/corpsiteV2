Anime key art, Shinji Aramaki style — hard-surface design, crisp panel lines, cel-shaded flat colour with sharp specular edges.

SETTING: a bright daytime future Tokyo street. The RIGHT HALF of the frame, from top edge to bottom edge, is a solid unbroken field of PURE BLACK — the void mouth of an alley — running off the right edge entirely. The left half is the bright street: pale concrete, glass, a blurred crowd passing.

THE FIGURE stands on the boundary between the two, and HE IS ONLY PARTIALLY DRAWN.

DRAWN — his left side only. Crisp, solid, brightly lit anime rendering: the left half of a LONG oversized bone-white hooded parka with the hem well below the hip toward the knee, worn open over an offset pale mid-layer and a high-collared under-layer; the left arm ending in a matte black cybernetic hand with narrow cyan-turquoise hairline channels; the left half of the hood. THE FACE IS A COMPLETE VOID — pure flat black inside the hood, no skin, no jaw, no eyes.

NOT DRAWN — his entire right side and both legs below mid-thigh. DO NOT DRAW THEM. Where his right shoulder, right arm, right hip and lower legs would be, there is simply THE BLACK FIELD. No silhouette, no outline, no edge, no contour, no suggestion of a limb. The black of the alley occupies that space directly.

THE TRANSITION between drawn and undrawn is WIDE, DIAGONAL and RAGGED. It runs from his right shoulder down across the body to his left knee — never a vertical line, never a straight edge, never an even gradient. Along it the white cloth breaks up unevenly into rough irregular dry-brush marks, some patches still bright white well into the dark zone, some already black high up on the lit side, folds and panel lines dropping out at different rates. It should look like the drawing was abandoned mid-stroke, not like a gradient was applied to a garment.

Both legs are entirely undrawn — from mid-thigh down there is only the black cast shadow running off the bottom of the frame.

ABSOLUTELY FORBIDDEN: tendrils, wisps, plumes, smoke, splatter, ribbons, tips, ends, or any ink shape that terminates in mid-air. There is no ink *object*. There is only a figure that has been drawn on one side and left undrawn on the other, with the black field filling the difference.

He reads as HALF-EMERGED from the shadow, mid-transition — becoming solid on the lit side.
