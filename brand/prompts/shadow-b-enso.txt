A sumi-e ink painting on a plain warm off-white paper ground, wide composition.

THE FIGURE, small and off-centre: a lone swordsman caught mid-turn in a sweeping cutting movement, painted as a SOLID BLACK INK SILHOUETTE. He wears a wide conical straw hat (kasa) which completely conceals his head — no face visible at any angle. Long layered coat and robes flaring with the turn.

He is DELIBERATELY INDISTINCT: parts of him solid and hard-edged, other parts unresolved and trailing into raw calligraphic brush strokes — the coat hem breaking into dry-brush tongues and curling tendrils, one arm dissolving into splatter, his feet never quite landing on the ground.

THE ENSO: an ENORMOUS single circular brush sweep encircles him, painted in one continuous gesture — thick and wet where it begins, thinning to dry-brush streaks and finally to nothing where it ends. It is many times his size and dominates the composition. Heavy black ink, with scattered flicked splatter following its arc.

THE WEAPON: the sword trails through the lower part of the circle. Around and BEHIND the blade only, a burst of brilliant saturated CYAN-TEAL ink bleeding and splashing outward along the arc, with bright droplets. This is the only saturated colour in the image and it belongs to the blade alone.

The movement is DANCE-LIKE rather than violent — sweeping, extended, unhurried.

Color: warm off-white paper, deep near-black ink with a faint blue-green cast, grey wash, one brilliant cyan-teal at the blade. Nothing else.

Style: sumi-e brush painting, watercolour made motion. NOT a photograph, NOT 3D rendered, NOT anime.

No text, no letters, no calligraphy characters, no seals, no watermark.
