A sumi-e ink painting on a plain warm off-white paper ground, with vast empty space around the figure.

THE FIGURE: a lone swordsman standing, seen from behind and slightly to one side, painted almost entirely as a SOLID BLACK INK SILHOUETTE. He wears a wide conical straw hat (kasa) that completely shadows and conceals his head — no face is visible at all, at any angle. Below it, a long layered travelling coat and robes.

He is DELIBERATELY INDISTINCT. Some edges of him are solid, hard and confidently painted — the hat brim, one shoulder, the line of his back. Other parts are unresolved and trail away into raw calligraphic BRUSH STROKES: the hem of his coat breaks into long dry-brush tongues, tendrils and curling wisps; one sleeve dissolves into splatter and drifting ink droplets; the bottom of the figure never fully lands, fading into the paper. He is a person and a brush mark at the same time.

Ink behaviour: real sumi-e — wet pooling blacks, grey wash bleeding into damp paper, dry-brush texture with visible bristle streaks, and scattered flicked splatter.

THE WEAPON: he holds a long straight sword low and out to one side. Around and BEHIND the blade only, a burst of brilliant saturated CYAN-TEAL — luminous ink bleeding and splashing outward, with a few bright droplets. This is the single saturated colour anywhere in the image and it belongs to the blade alone. The figure himself carries no colour.

Behind, very pale: soft grey ink-wash mountains, low contrast, dissolving into the paper.

Color: warm off-white paper, deep near-black ink with a faint blue-green cast, soft grey wash, and one brilliant cyan-teal at the blade. Nothing else — no orange, no red, no gold.

Style: sumi-e brush painting, watercolour made motion, calm and unresolved. NOT a photograph, NOT 3D rendered, NOT anime cel shading.

No text, no letters, no calligraphy characters, no seals, no watermark.
