Anime key art, Shinji Aramaki style — hard-surface design, crisp panel lines, cel-shaded flat colour.

SETTING: the aftermath. A dim future-Tokyo service corridor of grey machine panelling, dead screens and cable runs, fine dust in the air, one weak cold overhead light. A solid unbroken field of PURE BLACK covers the entire corridor floor and the far end, running off the bottom and back of the frame. Something has already happened and it is over.

THE FIGURE stands in the middle distance facing away, and HE IS ONLY PARTIALLY DRAWN.

DRAWN — from the shoulders to the hip only. Crisp, solid, hard-edged anime rendering: a LONG oversized dark hooded parka over an offset mid-layer and high-collared under-layer, hard-shell panels, one matte black cybernetic arm with narrow cyan-turquoise hairline channels along the plating. Hood up, head slightly lowered. THE FACE IS A COMPLETE VOID.

NOT DRAWN — everything below the hip. DO NOT DRAW HIS LEGS. Where his lower body, legs and feet would be, there is simply THE BLACK FIELD covering the floor. No silhouette, no outline, no contour, no shadow-shape of a leg. The floor's black occupies that space directly and runs off the bottom of the frame. He does not stand ON the black — he becomes it.

THE TRANSITION at the hip is a WIDE soft band — the parka's folds and panel lines fading out and the value stepping down into pure black over a generous distance. Gradual. No hard edge.

ABSOLUTELY FORBIDDEN: tendrils, wisps, plumes, smoke, splatter, ribbons, tips, ends, or any shape terminating in mid-air. No ink object of any kind. A figure drawn above the hip and left undrawn below it, with the black field filling the difference.

He reads as EMERGING from the floor's shadow, still half unformed.

Roughly seventy-five percent of the frame is near-black. No bodies, no gore, no damage — only emptiness and dust.
Color: near-black, cold machine grey, faint steel, narrow cyan-turquoise.
Style: cel-shaded anime. Quiet, cold, ominous. NOT photorealistic.
No text, no readable letters, no watermark, NO FACE, no blood, no bodies.
