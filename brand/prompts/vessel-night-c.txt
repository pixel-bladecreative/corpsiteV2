A single sculptural object photographed against a near-black background. The object is a large upright crescent: a thick disc of matte cast concrete with a second disc subtracted from it, leaving an open crescent standing on a dark surface. The concrete is fine-grained, matte and non-reflective, dark graphite, with visible surface tooth. Its concave inner face is scooped and catches a single low raking light from the upper left as a warm bronze band; the outer shoulder falls away into near-total darkness. One narrow near-white specular line marks the lit rim. Deep shadow, most of the frame holding no detail at all.

Cradled in the crescent's cup sits a small staged landscape: a low dark rock outcrop, and a bare wind-bent tree with fine dark branches and a scatter of small ember-lit leaves, reaching left out through the crescent opening. A tiny seated figure in a broad flat conical straw hat rests on the ledge at the left, in silhouette, head lowered, hands empty, very small in the frame.

Nocturnal, restrained, physical. More than half the frame is unlit near-black ground. Warm colour appears only where the light touches the pine and the inner face. No text, no lettering, no cherry blossom, no torii gate, no calligraphy, no seal stamps, no mountain, no glossy bevel, no outline, no glow around the silhouette.
