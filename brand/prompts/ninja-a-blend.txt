Anime key art in the style of Shinji Aramaki — hard-surface design, crisp panel lines, cel-shaded flat colour zones with sharp specular edges.

THE SETTING, and it is chosen for one reason: a bright daytime future Tokyo street, and the figure stands at the MOUTH OF A DARK ALLEY. To his right and behind him is a deep black void of alley shadow, running from the top of the frame to the bottom and off the right edge entirely. The bright street with its blurred passing crowd is to his left. A hard black cast shadow lies on the pavement beneath him and runs off the bottom of the frame.

THE FIGURE: a young cyber-ninja standing motionless, heavily layered — a LONG oversized bone-white hooded parka, hem well below the hip toward the knee, worn open over a second offset pale mid-layer, a high-collared under-layer, and stacked cargo trousers. Sealed seams, webbing straps, small hard-shell panels.

THE FACE IS A COMPLETE VOID — pure flat black inside the hood. No skin, no jaw, no eyes, nothing.

THE EFFECT — this is the entire point of the image:
His LEFT side is crisp, solid, brightly lit bone-white — clean anime linework, fully rendered.
Moving rightward across his body, the rendering DEGRADES CONTINUOUSLY: the white cloth loses its edges, breaks into rough dry-brush strokes, darkens, and by the time it reaches his right shoulder and right side it has become pure black — AND THAT BLACK IS THE SAME BLACK AS THE ALLEY SHADOW BEHIND HIM. There is NO OUTLINE, NO BOUNDARY and NO EDGE between his dissolving side and the alley darkness. They are one continuous field of black. You cannot tell where he stops and the shadow begins.
His legs likewise lose definition downward and merge seamlessly into the black cast shadow on the pavement, which runs off the bottom of the frame.

CRITICAL: NOTHING terminates in mid-air. There are NO tendrils, NO wisps with visible tips, NO floating ink shapes, NO smoke plumes, NO detached ribbons. Every dissolving part runs continuously INTO the alley shadow or OFF THE FRAME EDGE. He is not emitting anything. He is HALF-EMERGED from the shadow — becoming solid on the lit side, becoming shadow on the dark side.
