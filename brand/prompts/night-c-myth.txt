Anime key art in the style of Shinji Aramaki — hard-surface mechanical design, crisp panel lines, cel-shaded with flat colour zones and sharp specular edges.

THE MOMENT: the aftermath. A dim future-Tokyo service corridor of grey machine panelling, hard geometric plating, dead screens and cable runs. Something has already happened here and it is completely over. Fine dust hangs in the air. Nothing moves.

THE FIGURE, standing alone in the middle distance, facing away from the viewer: the young cyber-ninja, HEAVILY LAYERED — a LONG oversized dark hooded parka with the hem falling well BELOW THE HIP toward the knee, a second offset mid-layer jacket beneath it, a high-collared under-layer, loose cargo trousers stacked at the ankle, webbing straps and hard-shell panels over matte black articulated plating. Layers upon layers, each hem at a different length. Nothing cropped short.

Hood up. Head slightly lowered. THE FACE IS A COMPLETE VOID — no skin, no features, nothing but black inside the hood. His posture is relaxed and unhurried, arms at his sides, one matte black cybernetic hand hanging open. He is utterly still, and the stillness is the threat.

TECH: narrow cyan-turquoise hairline channels along spine plating and knuckles — the only saturated colour in the frame and the only thing that reads as alive.

THE DISSOLUTION: LARGE REGIONS of him are gone. His entire lower body below the waist, one full arm, and a broad section of his back have stopped being solid and become churning WET BLACK INK and smoky shadow — big torn ribbons and curling masses spilling outward across the corridor floor. Not a wisp, not a point source: whole parts of him simply are not there. A second, fainter mass of the same ink hangs further down the corridor, as though he arrived from there.

SETTING: hard grey machine panelling, deep shadow, one weak cold overhead light far above. Roughly seventy-five percent of the frame is near-black. No bodies, no gore, no visible damage — only emptiness and dust.

Color: near-black, cold machine grey, faint steel, narrow cyan-turquoise light. Nothing else.

Style: Shinji Aramaki anime, cel-shaded, hard-surface. Quiet, cold, ominous. NOT photorealistic.

No text, no readable letters, no watermark, no logo, NO FACE, no blood, no bodies.
