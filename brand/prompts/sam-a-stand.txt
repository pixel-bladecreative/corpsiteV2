A hyper-realistic photograph of a figure, standing, on a plain warm off-white paper ground — EXCEPT that parts of him are dissolving into wet black ink.

THE SOLID PARTS — these must be PHOTOREALISTIC, tack sharp, with total material fidelity, exactly as a real photograph of real cloth would look:
  - a wide conical woven hat, dark and weathered, every straw fibre and split visible, water-stained, its edge frayed
  - heavy layered robes: a coarse black outer over-robe, torn and ragged at the shoulder, its weave and broken threads clearly readable; beneath it a warm OCHRE-MUSTARD silk under-robe with real sheen, real creases, real drape and weight
  - a long string of heavy dark wooden prayer beads, each bead individually rendered with grain and wear
  - a forearm and hand of real weathered skin, tendons and knuckles sharp
  - cord and cloth wrappings at the wrist, individual fibres visible
Every solid surface reads as a real object under real light. Nothing painted, nothing illustrated.

THE FACE: completely lost in deep black shadow beneath the hat brim. No features are visible at all — only darkness. A single small warm AMBER light glows from within that shadow, low and dim, like a coal. That is the only warm light in the image.

THE DISSOLUTION — the ONLY stylised element: the lower half of his robes, one entire sleeve, and the trailing edge of the outer garment stop being cloth and become WET BLACK SUMI INK — long dry-brush tongues, curling tendrils, splatter and drifting droplets that fade into the paper. The transition is abrupt in places and gradual in others. Where he is solid he is a photograph; where he is not, he is a brush stroke. He never fully lands on the ground.

THE WEAPON: in his solid hand, a long straight sword. Around and BEHIND the blade only, a burst of brilliant saturated CYAN-TURQUOISE ink bleeding and splashing outward. This is the only saturated colour and it belongs to the blade alone.

Color: warm off-white paper, deep black, warm ochre-mustard, weathered skin, one small amber light, one cyan-turquoise burst.

Style: photorealistic figure meeting sumi-e ink dissolution. NOT a full illustration, NOT anime, NOT a flat silhouette. The realism is what makes the dissolution read.

No text, no letters, no calligraphy, no seals, no watermark, no gas mask, no respirator.
