A vast black exhibition hall, photographed straight on with a strong central perspective. A constructed installation.

Standing free on the floor in a receding series: enormous freestanding curved SCREENS, many storeys tall, each printed with a flat stylized landscape — layered mountain ridges, a few sparse pines, drawn as flat graphic shapes with no photographic texture at all, in deep blue-green teal on pale ground. The screens have no visible supports. Their curved vertical edges glow with a thin warm bronze light.

Running away from the viewer along the floor: a narrow pale walkway, and beside it a "river" that is clearly a lit graphic printed into the floor rather than real water, in deep teal.

THREE HUMAN FIGURES walk along the walkway at different distances, all seen from behind, all rendered TINY — the nearest no more than four percent of the frame height.

Above and around: total black void. No ceiling. The screens dissolve into darkness at the top of the frame.

Depth is built entirely from stacked flat planes, each one hazier and lower in contrast than the one in front.

Color: deep void black, deep blue-green teal, warm bronze edge glow, pale bone walkway. Absolutely nothing else — no coral, no red, no orange, no pink lotus, no cherry blossom.

Style: constructed, monumental, silent, and strange. A zen garden as imagined by a digital artist rather than as it exists. Calm but impossible.

No text, no letters, no calligraphy, no watermark, no logo.
