An architectural model FLOATING in a vast empty field of warm paper-white. There is no table, no desk, no floor, no room — the model hangs weightless in bright empty space, surrounded by enormous emptiness on all sides.

THE STRUCTURE: a cantilevered building in the spirit of Frank Lloyd Wright and Tadao Ando. Strict ninety-degree angles only. Long horizontal slabs stacked and offset, reaching far out into empty air with nothing beneath them. Deep overhangs. One tall narrow slot cut through the mass. Serene, geometric, weightless.

MATERIALS — hand-made and honestly textured, seen close and sharp: board-formed concrete rendered in fine matte grey card with a faint tooth; warm wood veneer with real visible grain; hairline brushed steel edges catching a thin bright line; and terraces built from layers of matte CONSTRUCTION PAPER in burnt rust and deep teal, cut clean with slightly fibrous edges, each layer casting a real shallow shadow onto the one below.

Warm amber light glows out of the window slots. It is the only warm light.

THE DRAFTING LAYER: fine charcoal and thin rust-red hairlines — construction lines, dimension lines, a light grid — drawn over and around the structure. They extend FAR out past the building into the empty white, running most of the way across the frame, and DISSOLVE TO NOTHING before reaching the edges. Small tick marks and node dots sit along them. A few pale soft-edged grey circles float behind, like clouds seen through paper.

Color: warm paper white, matte charcoal, warm wood tan, deep teal, burnt rust, one amber glow.

Style: dreamlike, weightless, precise and achingly beautiful. A dream of the future built as a physical object. NOT cyberpunk, NOT neon, NOT sci-fi, no holograms, no vehicles, no people, no tabletop photography.

No text, no readable letters, no watermark, no logo.
