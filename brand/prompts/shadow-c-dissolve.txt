A sumi-e ink painting on a plain warm off-white paper ground, showing ONE figure caught mid-teleport — dissolving in one place and re-forming in another.

ON THE RIGHT: the swordsman is COALESCING back into being. His wide conical straw hat (kasa) and his shoulders are already solid black ink, hard-edged and confident — no face visible beneath the hat. Below the chest he is not yet resolved: his coat and legs are still a rushing spiral of inky wisps, ribbons and streaming brush strokes gathering into shape.

ON THE LEFT, where he was: only the RESIDUE remains — a slow-turning vortex of black ink swirls, curling tendrils, drifting droplets and pale grey wash, still holding the faint suggestion of a standing figure that is no longer there.

BETWEEN THEM: a long sweeping trail of inky wisp connects the two — a rushing horizontal current of ribbon-like brush strokes, splatter and smoke-like grey wash, showing the path he travelled.

Ink behaviour: real sumi-e — wet pooling blacks, grey wash bleeding into damp paper, dry-brush bristle streaks, flicked splatter.

THE WEAPON: in his re-forming hand, a long straight sword, and around and BEHIND the blade only a burst of brilliant saturated CYAN-TEAL ink bleeding outward. This is the only saturated colour in the image.

Vast empty paper above and below. The whole image is calm and dreamlike, not violent.
