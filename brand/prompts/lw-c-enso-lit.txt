A vast dark stage set, photographed straight on. A constructed installation in a black void.

Filling the upper two thirds: an ENORMOUS perfect circle, many storeys tall, standing free with no visible support — a translucent screen lit from behind.

INSIDE THE CIRCLE, glowing as though back-projected: a lush saturated landscape in flat graphic layers — coral and vermilion maple canopies, a warm rose and amber sky, burnt-orange ridgelines, and blue-green teal mountains receding behind, each rank paler and hazier than the one in front. Rich, warm and luminous, like a lit transparency.

Standing in front of the circle in complete silhouette: a single old pine, trunk leaning, branches spreading in flat horizontal layers. Pure black shape, no interior detail.

At the base, on a low dark plinth, TWO HUMAN FIGURES seen from behind, TINY — no more than three percent of the frame height, in full silhouette.

The floor is a seamless black mirror doubling the disc, the tree and the figures.

CRITICAL: everything outside the circle is deep void black with only the faintest bronze rim on the disc's edge. The saturated color is contained entirely within the circle and does not bloom into the surrounding void.

Style: constructed, staged, still and strange. Calm but impossible. Cinematic.

No text, no letters, no calligraphy, no watermark, no logo.
