A single sculptural object photographed against a warm off-white paper background. An upright open crescent cut from one thick disc of matte dark concrete, standing on a pale surface, lit by one low raking light from the upper left. The concave inner face is scooped and catches the light as a pale band; the outer shoulder falls into deep shadow; a narrow near-white line marks the lit rim. Short soft contact shadow.

Held in the crescent's cup is a still black pool of water with a dark stone ledge across it. A small worn rock rises from the water on the right, carrying a low windswept pine with flat horizontal pads of foliage in muted rust. On the ledge at the left sits a tiny figure in a broad flat conical straw hat, silhouetted, head lowered, hands empty, very small in the frame. A faint reflection of the pine lies on the water.

Restrained and quiet. More than half the frame is empty pale ground. Colour appears only in the pine. No text, no lettering, no cherry blossom, no torii gate, no calligraphy, no seal stamps, no mountain, no glossy bevel, no outline.
