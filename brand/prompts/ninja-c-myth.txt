Anime key art in the style of Shinji Aramaki — hard-surface design, crisp panel lines, cel-shaded flat colour zones.

THE SETTING: the aftermath. A dim future-Tokyo service corridor of grey machine panelling, dead screens and cable runs. Fine dust hangs in the air. One weak cold overhead light far above. The lower third of the frame and the entire far end of the corridor are a solid unbroken field of near-black that runs off the bottom and back of the frame. Something has already happened here and it is over.

THE FIGURE, standing in the middle distance facing away, heavily layered — a LONG oversized dark hooded parka, hem well below the hip toward the knee, over an offset mid-layer, a high-collared under-layer and stacked cargo trousers, with hard-shell panels over matte black plating. Hood up, head slightly lowered. THE FACE IS A COMPLETE VOID. Posture relaxed, arms at his sides, one matte black cybernetic hand hanging open. Utterly still.

THE EFFECT — the entire point of the image:
His upper body — shoulders, hood, the lit arm — is crisp, solid, hard-edged cel-shaded anime.
From roughly the waist down the rendering DISSOLVES CONTINUOUSLY: fabric folds soften, edges break into rough dry-brush strokes, values darken, and his lower body MERGES SEAMLESSLY INTO THE BLACK FIELD covering the corridor floor. There is NO OUTLINE and NO BOUNDARY where he ends — his darkness and the floor's darkness are the same black, one continuous field running off the bottom of the frame.
Behind him, further down the corridor, a second broad passage of the same unbroken black spreads across the floor and up one wall, running off the back of the frame — as though he arrived through it.

CRITICAL: NOTHING terminates in mid-air. NO tendrils, NO wisps, NO plumes, NO floating shapes with tips or ends. He is not emitting shadow — HE IS EMERGING FROM IT, still half unformed.

Narrow cyan-turquoise hairline channels on the spine plating and knuckles — the only saturated colour and the only thing that reads as alive.

Roughly seventy-five percent of the frame is near-black. No bodies, no gore, no visible damage — only emptiness and dust.

Color: near-black, cold machine grey, faint steel, narrow cyan-turquoise. Nothing else.

Style: Shinji Aramaki anime, cel-shaded. Quiet, cold, ominous. NOT photorealistic.

No text, no readable letters, no watermark, NO FACE, no blood, no bodies.
