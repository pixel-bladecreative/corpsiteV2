Cinematic vertical composition. A vast dark interior void that could equally be a night landscape — the boundary between architecture and weather is deliberately unresolved.

Suspended in the void, with no visible cable, support or explanation: an enormous origami paper crane, folded from pale bone-white paper, the size of a cathedral. It hangs slightly nose-down, utterly still. Its fold lines catch a thin warm gold light along every crease. The paper is matte, real, slightly fibrous, with visible weight and subtle sag — a real folded object, not a rendering of one.

Behind and beside it, receding into blackness, three or four more colossal folded paper forms at diminishing scales, each one hazier and lower in contrast than the one in front, dissolving into the dark.

At the very bottom of the frame, standing on a black mirror-still floor of water, a single human figure seen from behind, in dark clothing — rendered TINY, no more than two percent of the frame height. The figure is the only thing in the image at human scale, and it establishes that everything else is impossible.

The floor reflects the crane and the light in a perfect, unbroken, glassy mirror.

Light: overwhelmingly dark, roughly eighty-five percent of the frame is deep black. A single warm amber source rakes across the folded paper from one side. Volumetric haze separates each depth plane.

Color: dead flat black, warm bone-white paper, tan-leaning matte gold (#C5A05F) on the fold lines, warm amber light. Deeply restrained and nearly monochrome — no saturated colors, no blues, no reds, no teal-and-orange grade.

Camera: vertical portrait orientation, wide lens, low viewpoint looking up, extreme depth. Fine natural film grain. Photographic realism with the stillness of a painting.

No text, no letters, no calligraphy, no watermark, no logo, no signature, no stamp.
