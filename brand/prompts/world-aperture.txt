A view into a dreamed Japanese landscape at night, meant to be seen through a circular
window. Layered planes receding into mist: a near ridge in near-black silhouette, a
middle range of mountains in cool deep petrol blue, a far range dissolving to almost
nothing. Low fog lies in the valleys between them in flat horizontal bands. Above, a
vast quiet sky. A single warm bronze light source low on the horizon — a distant lantern
or a temple window — throws a narrow warm reflection across still water at the base.
Extremely restrained colour: near-black ground, cool petrol blues, one warm bronze
accent used once. No moon disc, no sun disc, no red, no figures, no buildings in the
foreground, no text. Calm, strange, patient. Cinematic, deep atmospheric perspective.
