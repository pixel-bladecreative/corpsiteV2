A vertical night scene of enormous illuminated hanging forms suspended in a deep dark void. Constructed, staged, impossible — not a real place.

SUSPENDED WITHOUT SUPPORT, at many different depths and sizes: tall vertical hanging paper scroll panels, some many storeys high, and large round and cylindrical paper lanterns. Each is lit from within and glows brilliantly. The nearest are cropped by the frame edge; the furthest are small and hazy.

THE GLOWING SURFACES ARE RICHLY SATURATED — this is a jewelled image. Across the panels and lanterns: deep vermilion red, warm coral, golden amber, saturated cyan-teal, and warm ivory. Each panel carries a sparse painted landscape — pine branches, a ridgeline, blossom — in fine gold and ink line work. Vivid, luminous, almost lacquered.

CRITICAL — THE GROUND STAYS DARK: everything that is not itself a light source is deep near-black navy. The void between and behind the hanging forms is unlit and bottomless. The saturated elements are DISCRETE OBJECTS separated by large areas of darkness. The image must never become a bright field — it must read as bright jewels scattered in a dark room. At least two thirds of the frame carries no light at all.

At the bottom, standing on a black mirror-still water surface, ONE human figure seen from behind, rendered TINY at roughly two percent of the frame height, in dark silhouette.

The water is a perfect unbroken mirror, doubling the lanterns and panels downward as long soft coloured reflections.

Fine atmospheric haze separates each depth plane.

Style: constructed, silent, calm and strange. Maximum colour, minimum light. Cinematic, high-end.

No text, no letters, no calligraphy, no readable writing, no watermark, no logo, no stamp, no red seal.
