An exploded axonometric of a building, FLOATING in a vast empty field of warm paper-white. No table, no ground, no room — enormous empty space on every side.

THE STRUCTURE: a Tadao Ando and Frank Lloyd Wright inspired composition separated into its horizontal layers, each slab lifted clear of the one below and held apart in mid-air, so the whole building reads as a stack of floating planes. Strict ninety-degree angles only. Long cantilevers reaching into nothing.

MATERIALS, honestly textured and sharply seen: matte grey board-formed concrete card, warm wood veneer with visible grain, thin brushed steel plates catching hairline highlights, and slabs of matte CONSTRUCTION PAPER in burnt rust and deep teal with clean slightly fibrous cut edges. Each floating layer casts a soft real shadow into the space beneath it.

Fine amber light spills from between two of the layers.

THE DRAFTING LAYER: hairline charcoal and thin rust-red construction lines run vertically between the separated layers, showing how they would assemble. They continue FAR beyond the structure in every direction, out into the empty white, and FADE COMPLETELY TO NOTHING before they reach the frame edges. Small dimension ticks, node dots and tiny scale marks sit along them.

Color: warm paper white, matte charcoal, warm wood tan, deep teal, burnt rust, one amber glow. Restrained.

Style: dreamlike, weightless, elegant, precise. Engineering rendered as poetry. NOT cyberpunk, NOT neon, NOT sci-fi, no holograms, no glow effects, no people.

No text, no readable letters, no watermark, no logo.
