A single minimalist logo symbol in the tradition of an authentic Japanese kamon (family crest), centered on a completely flat non-metallic dead black background (#0A0A0A).

The symbol: a crane radically reduced and contained within a perfect thin circle — the historical "tsuru no maru" crane-in-a-circle construction. Extreme reduction: the crane is abstracted into a few bold flat geometric shapes with perfect radial symmetry, wings sweeping into the circle, the whole form reading as a seal rather than a picture of a bird. The containing circle is a thin uniform ring.

Color: one flat tan-leaning old gold (#C6A664) on flat black. Solid flat shapes and one hairline ring — no gradient, no shading, no glow, no bevel, no metallic reflection, no drop shadow, no texture.

Composition: perfectly centered, perfectly symmetrical, generous empty black margin. Flat vector aesthetic, crisp, print-quality, the weight and authority of a real heraldic crest — clean and formal, not busy or ornamental.

No text, no letters, no kanji, no numbers, no watermark, no border beyond the circle itself.
