An achingly beautiful architectural diorama, photographed straight on, floating on a plain warm paper-white ground.

THE SUBJECT: a small cantilevered building in the spirit of Frank Lloyd Wright and Tadao Ando — strict ninety-degree angles, long horizontal slabs stacked and offset so they reach out into empty space, deep overhangs, a tall narrow slot of light cut through the mass. Serene, geometric, monumental in feeling but physically small.

IT IS A HAND-BUILT SCALE MODEL, and the materials read as real: board-formed concrete in fine matte grey card, warm wood veneer with visible grain, thin brushed steel edges, and layers of matte CONSTRUCTION PAPER in warm rust and deep teal — cut cleanly, slightly fibrous at the cut edge, stacked to make terrain and terraces. Real shallow shadows fall between the layers.

INSIDE, warm amber light glows out of the window slots — the only warm light in the image.

THE DRAFTING LAYER — critical: fine hairline construction lines, dimension lines and a light grid are drawn over and around the model in charcoal and thin rust red. They extend well PAST the building, far out into the empty paper, and FADE TO NOTHING before reaching the edges of the frame. A few small annotation marks and tick scales sit along them.

Behind and around: generous empty warm paper-white space with a faint soft texture, and a few pale grey soft-edged circles floating like clouds.

Color: warm paper white, matte charcoal grey, warm wood tan, deep teal, burnt rust orange, and one amber glow. Restrained and elegant.

Style: dreamlike, calm, precise. A dream of the future rendered as a physical model. NOT cyberpunk, NOT neon, NOT sci-fi, no glowing blue holograms, no futuristic vehicles, no crowds.

No text, no readable letters, no watermark, no logo, no people.
