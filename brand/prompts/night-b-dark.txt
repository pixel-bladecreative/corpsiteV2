Anime key art in the style of Shinji Aramaki — hard-surface mechanical design, crisp panel lines, cel-shaded with flat colour zones and sharp specular edges.

THE FIGURE: the same young cyber-ninja at night in a rain-wet future Tokyo alley, and his clothing has CHANGED TO DARK. He is HEAVILY LAYERED:
  - a LONG oversized black hooded parka, hem falling well BELOW THE HIP toward the knee, worn open, matte and light-drinking, heavy folds
  - beneath it a second charcoal mid-layer jacket, also long, offset from the first
  - a high-collared black under-layer
  - loose dark cargo trousers stacked at the ankle
  - sealed seams, webbing straps, hard-shell panels at shoulder and forearm over matte black articulated plating
Layers upon layers, each hem at a different length. Nothing cropped short.

THE FACE IS A COMPLETE VOID. Hood up and pulled forward, and inside it pure flat black — no skin, no jaw, no features of any kind. Absolute.

He stands upright and still on the wet ground, weight even, head slightly turned. Not attacking. Not crouching. Simply present.

TECH: one matte black cybernetic arm with articulated joints. Narrow cyan-turquoise hairline channels along plating and knuckles — thin, hard-edged, restrained.

THE DISSOLUTION — critical: HALF OF HIM IS GONE. His entire right side — the whole arm, that shoulder, that half of the parka and the ribs beneath it — has stopped existing as a body and become a violent churning mass of WET BLACK INK and torn shadow: large curling ribbons, ragged tendrils and drifting droplets bleeding out into the alley. The boundary where the body ends and the ink begins is ragged and runs right through his torso. This is NOT smoke rising from a point and NOT a wisp at the shoulder — it is a whole region of him replaced by shadow.

SETTING: a narrow rain-wet future Tokyo alley at night. Deep black dominates. Distant signage dim and heavily desaturated, barely readable, never a neon blaze. Wet reflections. Roughly seventy percent of the frame carries no detail.

Color: deep black, charcoal, wet grey, narrow cyan-turquoise light. No magenta, no hot pink, no orange neon.

Style: Shinji Aramaki anime, cel-shaded, hard-surface. NOT photorealistic.

No text, no readable letters, no watermark, no logo, NO FACE, no skin visible.
