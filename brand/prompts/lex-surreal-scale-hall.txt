Cinematic vertical composition. A colossal hall that cannot exist — the space has no ceiling, no visible walls, and dissolves into black void at every edge.

Hanging in the void, in a wide arc receding into depth, a series of ENORMOUS vertical hanging paper scrolls, each many storeys tall, suspended without visible support. The scrolls are matte bone-white paper. Each carries a sparse ink-drawn landscape — a few pine branches, a distant ridge line, mist — drawn with extreme restraint and enormous empty space, in near-black ink with occasional thin gold linework. The nearest scrolls are cropped by the frame edge; the furthest are small, hazy, and almost lost.

At the base, on a seamless black mirror-polished floor, two or three human figures in dark clothing, walking, rendered TINY — each no more than three percent of the frame height. They are the only human-scale objects present.

The floor is a perfect unbroken mirror, doubling the scrolls and the light into the bottom of the frame.

Light: predominantly darkness. Shafts of warm light fall between the scrolls, made visible by fine atmospheric haze, each shaft weaker and cooler than the last as it recedes. Deep black in the gaps between planes.

Color: dead flat black, warm bone-white paper, tan-leaning matte gold (#C5A05F) in the linework and light, warm amber haze. Nearly monochrome and severely restrained — absolutely no saturated color, no blue, no red, no teal-and-orange grade.

Camera: vertical portrait orientation, wide lens, eye level, extreme sense of depth with many clearly separated planes. Fine natural film grain. Photographic realism, monumental and silent.

No text, no letters, no calligraphy, no readable writing, no watermark, no logo, no signature, no stamp.
