A constructed installation in a vast dark void, with a single figure standing in it. Staged, impossible, silent.

THE ENVIRONMENT: a seamless black mirror floor stretching away, and standing free upon it with no visible support, several ENORMOUS luminous vertical columns many storeys tall, glowing soft warm bronze with fine vertical striations, their edges catching razor-thin near-white lines. Behind them, flat stacked graphic mountain planes in deep blue-green teal recede into haze, each rank paler than the one before. Total black void above. No ceiling.

THE FIGURE, small in the frame at roughly a fifth of its height, standing between two columns: a lean shaven-headed monk, a fictional character, STANDING STILL — feet planted, arms hanging loose and slightly away from the body, head tilted down, eyes closed, face completely calm. He is not posing and not fighting. NO WEAPON.

BUT HE READS AS MOVING. His many-layered robes — a bone linen under-robe, a coarse warm grey-tan hemp over-robe with a hem torn into long ragged tongues, a wound rope sash, frayed cloth wrappings at forearm and shin — lift and stream sideways as though caught in a wind that is not there. The torn hems trail out behind him and break apart into fine drifting particles and pale dust that dissolve into the dark. A few sweeping calligraphic ink ribbons in warm bronze and deep teal arc around him, drawn as wet brush strokes with tapered ends, describing motion he is not making.

He is sharp and in focus, never motion-blurred. The stillness of the body against the movement of everything around it is the entire point of the image.

The mirror floor doubles the columns and the figure downward.

Color: deep void black, warm bronze, deep blue-green teal, bone, warm grey-tan, rope brown.

Style: dreamlike, constructed, calm and strange. NOT a photograph of a real person, NOT a film still, no modern clothing.

No text, no letters, no calligraphy characters, no seals, no watermark.
