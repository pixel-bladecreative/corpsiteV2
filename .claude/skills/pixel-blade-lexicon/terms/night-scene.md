# night scene

Tagged references: `focus-poster`, `quietlab`, `auraplan`, `night-scene` plate
Brand-native plate: `plates/night-scene.png`

## What Spencer means by it

The **photographic condition**. A specific lighting situation, independent of
mood and of layout — `auraplan`'s lit office is a night scene and is not
brooding at all.

## Mechanics

1. **One light source, and it is warm.** Not two, not ambient fill. A single
   practical — a lantern, a doorway, a screen, the last of the sun — and the
   frame organises itself around where that light reaches.

2. **Hard warm/cool split.** Warm amber where the source lands, cool blue-grey
   where it does not, black in between. This split does most of the work; it is
   what makes a night image read as *night* rather than as underexposed.

3. **Fast falloff, clean shadows.** Light dies within a short distance. Shadows
   crush to true black and stay clean — never lifted, never muddy, never a grey
   haze standing in for darkness.

4. **70–85% of the frame carries no detail.** Measured, not felt. This is what
   leaves room for type and gold linework to sit on top without a scrim.

5. **Visible atmosphere.** Haze, mist, or fine rain, catching the light where it
   crosses the beam and invisible elsewhere. This is also what separates depth
   planes — see `surreal`.

6. **No colored practicals.** One warm source. No neon, no colored gels, no
   teal-and-orange grade. `focus-poster`'s amber sunset against slate cloud is
   the widest the temperature range should ever get.

## Borrow / don't borrow

**Borrow:** all of it. This transfers wholesale and is already how the round-one
world plates were shot.

**Do not borrow:** `focus-poster`'s saturation in the sunset. It runs hotter and
more orange than our amber. Same structure, dial the saturation back.

## Relationship to the palette

A night scene is the **garden** layer. Its colors are `--pb-andon`,
`--pb-yoi`, `--pb-koke`, `--pb-ishi` — never `--pb-kin`. Gold appears on top of
a night scene as linework and type, never inside it as light.
