# dark elegance

Tagged references: `focus-poster`, `quietlab`, `auraplan`, `glasshaven` (partial)
Brand-native exemplar: `plates/dark-elegance-hero.html` (real code, not an image)

## What Spencer means by it

The **treatment**, not the mood and not the photography — those are `brooding`
and `night scene`. This is how the interface behaves on top of them.

Near-black ground. One warm accent, used once per view. Light-weight type at
large size. Hairlines instead of boxes. Enormous negative space. Nothing raises
its voice, and the restraint is what reads as expensive.

The failure mode is a dark theme that is merely *dark* — same busy layout,
inverted. Elegance here is subtraction.

## Mechanics

1. **Hierarchy by tracking and opacity, not weight.** `focus-poster` sets
   FOCUS / DISCIPLINE / CONSISTENCY at nearly the same size, separating them by
   letter-spacing and brightness alone. Almost no bold anywhere in these
   references. Weight is the crude tool; tracking and value are the fine ones.

2. **One accent, one place.** `auraplan` puts the entire warm accent budget into
   a single word — "INTENTION" — inside an otherwise white headline, plus one
   filled button. Nothing else is gold. Spending the accent twice halves it.

3. **The primary/secondary button pair.** One solid accent button with dark text,
   one ghost button with a hairline border and light text. `auraplan` is exactly
   this. Our contrast math already supports it: sumi on kin measures 7.99:1.

4. **Type lives in the dark half of the photograph.** Never a scrim, never a
   blur-behind panel. Compose or crop the image so an unlit region exists, and
   put the type there. `auraplan`, `focus-poster` and `quietlab` all do this.

5. **Hairlines as the only structure.** Thin rules, vertical dividers between
   feature items, corner brackets. No filled cards, no borders that enclose, no
   shadows. `quietlab`'s corner brackets frame an image by marking four corners
   and nothing else.

6. **Quiet calls to action.** Low contrast, small, calm. `quietlab` pairs a muted
   filled button with a plain text link and a chevron. Confidence, not urgency.

7. **Sentence case is allowed at display size.** `quietlab` runs a large
   sentence-case serif-free headline. Tracked caps are not the only register —
   caps for labels and eyebrows, sentence case for the actual argument.

8. **Instrument marks.** `focus-poster` carries a plumb line with a dot node,
   contour arcs, a dot-matrix grid, edge tick marks. They suggest measurement and
   navigation, which is precisely the precision half of this brand. **Guardrail
   below.**

## The instrument-marks guardrail

Decorative HUD garnish is one of the loudest tells of generated design. The line:
**an instrument mark must encode something true or be structurally load-bearing.**
A tick scale that marks real section positions, a plumb line that is an actual
scroll-progress indicator, a dot node at a real anchor point — those earn their
place. The same marks sprinkled for texture do not. If it cannot be explained,
delete it.

## Borrow / don't borrow

**Borrow:** everything in the mechanics list. The `auraplan` reference in
particular is close to already being Pixel Blade — near-black, warm wood, gold
accent word, gold-plus-ghost button pair, a thin geometric mark beside a tracked
wordmark.

**Do not borrow from `glasshaven`:** rounded-corner cards floating on a slate
ground, and ultra-bold wide display type. Both contradict rules already set —
no shadows, one elevation step, hairlines over boxes, and gold-as-ink rather
than heavy contrast. Its *structure* transfers (see `patterns/`); its surface
does not.

**Do not borrow:** the muted mauve-brown grade of `quietlab`. Its restraint
transfers, its color temperature does not — ours is amber against blue-grey.
