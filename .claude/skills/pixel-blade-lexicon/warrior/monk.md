# Monk — final form

Plates: `plates/monk-paper.png` (day), `plates/monk-night.png`,
`plates/monk-garden.png` — all feathered and passing.
Reference: three images supplied 2026-08-25. Not stored on disk.

Spencer: *"He's no longer hiding from the sun. He's watercolor and mist and
motion. This figure should be able to look like he's in motion simply standing in
any of our environments, or fighting an Oni."*

This is the **evolution** of the character — his final form.

His cloth sits at hue 28–34, **saturation 5–45** — bronze's hue family worn well
below the brand's own 59. Compare `samurai.md`, whose cloth runs the same hue at
saturation 63–79. **Mastery is turning it down.**

His hands are empty, always. Not an absence — the student needs a weapon to show
what he might be, and the master does not.

## Two findings that decide how he integrates

### 1. He is made of the brand

His cloth, quantized, returns `#624B37` `#9D8671` `#C2946B` `#CEAF91` `#E0D3C2`
`#F1EFE6` — **every one at hue 28–34.**

Bronze is hue 29.

He is not a neutral guest brought in from outside. He is **the bronze family worn
down** — same hue, saturation dialled from 59 to somewhere between 5 and 45. That
is why he sits in these environments without looking composited.

### 2. Ground-agnosticism is a value ramp, not a vibe

| Cloth layer | On sumi #0A0A0B | On paper #F4F5F2 |
|---|---|---|
| shadow `#624B37` | 2.43 | **7.44** |
| rope `#9D8671` | 5.73 | 3.16 |
| hemp `#C2946B` | **7.30** | 2.48 |
| tan `#CEAF91` | **9.58** | 1.89 |
| worn `#E0D3C2` | **13.44** | 1.35 |
| linen `#F1EFE6` | **17.18** | 1.05 |

He contains both ends of the range. **On a dark ground his light layers carry
him; on a light ground his dark layers do.** No adaptation needed — which is
exactly why the source references work equally on cream, dark brown and
grey-blue.

This is the technical answer to "should be able to stand in any of our
environments."

## The wardrobe — the detail that matters

Never one garment. Distinct layers, each a different weave and a different value:

- **bone-white linen under-robe** — fine, soft, close to the body
- **coarse hemp over-robe** in warm grey-tan — stiff and sculptural, hem torn
  into long ragged tongues that fan with the movement
- **broad rope sash**, wound many times, dark rope-brown, dry and hard
- **wrapped strips** at forearm and shin — frayed cloth and cord
- an **ochre-tan panel** hanging from one shoulder

Each layer must catch light differently. If the cloth reads as one material, the
figure has failed — the layering is the character.

## Mechanics

1. **Sharp figure, moving surround.** He is never motion-blurred. The motion is
   in the ink ribbons, the dissolving particles, and cloth streaming in a wind
   that is not there. `monk-garden.png` is the proof: he is standing perfectly
   still and reads as mid-movement.
2. **Dissolution at the edges.** Torn hem, trailing sleeve, the away side —
   breaking into fine drifting particles and pale mist. Not fully contained.
3. **Face at rest.** Head tilted down, eyes closed, calm. No aggression, no
   strain. This is the single most fragile rule under generation — asking for
   "action" produces a shouting face every time.
4. **No weapon.** Hands empty and open, always.
5. **Dance before strike.** The pose reads as choreography as much as combat —
   sweeping, extended, weighted. Even the crouch is coiled rather than braced.
6. **Motion as brushwork.** Calligraphic ink ribbons with visible bristle texture
   and tapered ends, describing arcs his limbs have travelled. Charcoal, plus
   **petrol teal and bronze/rust** — this is the only place brand saturation
   touches a figure.

## The kasa — permitted at mark scale

Spencer, 2026-08-26: **the hat stays in this application.**

The Monk has no hat in his environment plates and does not get one there — the
broad hat remains the Samurai's, who is *always* hatted. But inside the mark his
wardrobe, his hue family and his empty hands are all invisible. At a tenth of
frame height the silhouette is the entire figure, and a broad flat conical kasa
is the only element that survives the reduction and still reads as a seated
person rather than a smudge.

**The rule.** The kasa is permitted when the Monk appears inside a mark or a
made object at roughly a tenth of frame height or smaller. It is forbidden in
environment plates and in portraits, where the layered wardrobe does the work
and a hat would flatten him toward the student.

This is a legibility exception and nothing in the mythos follows from it. The
Samurai's hat still means what it meant; the Monk has not acquired one. If a
future session finds this hat in a full-size plate, that is a bug.

## Borrow / don't borrow

**Borrow:** all of the above.

**Do not borrow the seals and calligraphy blocks** from the references. Same
ruling as `surreal` and `tech`: if type appears in this brand it is load-bearing,
not decoration.

**Do not let him become a film still.** The references sit near wuxia poster
territory. Ours stays in the dream — watercolour, mist, dissolution — per the
standing instruction on `tech`.

**Do not brighten the face.** Lighting him frontally kills it. He is lit from
one side with the shadow half falling away, in both modes.
