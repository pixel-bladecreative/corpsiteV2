# ref: staged-gardens

**Tags:** `dreamed garden`, `surreal`, `scale`
**Source:** five images supplied 2026-08-25. Not stored on disk.

1. **Moon-screen stage.** A giant backlit amber disc; a tree in full silhouette
   in front of it; two tiny figures on a pale plinth; black mirror floor;
   volumetric shafts from above. A stage set in a void.
2. **Light columns.** Free-standing luminous vertical columns of varied width and
   depth on a polished floor; beyond glass, a stylized blue-grey mountain
   landscape with coral maple; one tiny walking figure.
3. **Bonsai corridor.** A clean modern corridor, thin gold reveal lines, a
   massive sculpted pine on a black plinth with water falling from it, deep teal
   glass behind, skylight above, one small robed figure. Light ground.
4. **Snow courtyard.** A dark modern interior looking onto a courtyard where snow
   falls on sculpted pines. Near-monochrome, extremely calm. The least strange
   and the most "feel of the place."
5. **TAO exhibition.** Enormous curved free-standing screens printed with
   stylized shan-shui landscape; a lit graphic "river" in the floor; tiny figures
   on a pale walkway; coral screen edges against blue mountains.

## What they teach

The whole `dreamed garden` term. Collectively they say the garden must be
**constructed rather than found**, that landscape becomes **flat stacked graphic
planes**, that **light is an object** rather than an effect, and that a **mirror
floor** is standard equipment.

They also confirm the warm/cool engine maps onto bronze and petrol.

## Caution

- **Coral, red, pink** appear in three of the five. They do not transfer — bronze
  holds the warm role alone.
- **Reference 3 is a light-ground space.** Ours is dark.
- Reference 4 is the calmest and least strange; useful as the floor of the range,
  not the target.
