# ref: focus-poster

**Tags:** `dark-elegance`, `night-scene`, `brooding` (partial)
**Source:** supplied 2026-08-25. Not stored on disk.

Vertical poster. A winding road leads to a mountain under a dramatic amber
sunset; the top third is near-black sky used as type space.

`FOCUS` in tracked gold caps, then `DISCIPLINE` and `CONSISTENCY` beneath in
progressively tighter, dimmer white caps. A thin vertical plumb line with a dot
node drops from the top edge; a short gold rule separates. Bottom-left carries a
three-line micro-copy stack in small caps with the last line in gold. Edges
carry contour arcs, a dot-matrix grid, and tick marks.

**Teaches:** `tracked-stack`, `instrument-marks`, `dark-half-type`, and the
brooding copy register — short declaratives with the turn in the last line.

**Caution:** the sunset runs hotter and more saturated than our amber. Structure
transfers, saturation does not. The instrument marks here are decorative; ours
must be load-bearing.
