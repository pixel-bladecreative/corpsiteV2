# ref · enso-scene
**Tags:** `struck`, `dreamed garden`

A brush-drawn enso in black sumi, its stroke thick and dry-edged, opening at
the lower left where the ink runs out and drips. Inside the ring: grey wash
mountains, low mist, a small torii. Growing over and across the ring from
outside: a plum branch in red blossom that crosses the stroke and breaks the
circle's outline in three places. Three tiny birds leaving at the lower left.

**The mechanic that transfers.** The ring is not a container holding a picture
— the branch *crosses in front of it* and the ring passes *behind* the trunk.
Two depths, established purely by occlusion, in a completely flat medium. This
is the same move the mark already makes with the crane, and it confirms the
move rather than teaching a new one.

**The second mechanic.** The ring's interior is a *world*, not a fill. The
circle is a window onto somewhere. This became the `aperture` mode.

**Don't borrow:** the torii, the blossom, the drips. The drips in particular —
they are a wet-media accident, and this brand's edge language is a square cut
or a pixel, not a run.
