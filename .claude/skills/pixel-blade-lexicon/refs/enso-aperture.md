# ref · enso-aperture
**Tags:** `struck`, `surreal`, `scale`, `brooding`

A perfect ring on a pale warm ground. The ring's *stroke itself* is filled with
photography — a sky going from deep red at the top through to near-black,
with cloud banks running through it. Inside the ring is empty pale ground. Low
mist across the base. A single small seated figure far below, maybe 3% of frame
height, with incense smoke.

**The mechanic, and it is the best one in the set.** The shape is not drawn in
a colour. It is **cut**, and there is a world behind the page. The ring stops
being an object and becomes an absence.

**Also:** the figure is doing `scale`'s job exactly — tiny, turned away,
establishing that the ring is enormous.

**Borrow:** the cut. Two modes came out of it — `aperture` (world through the
ring's interior) and `inlay` (the mark's own strokes are the only window). The
inlay reads best in day mode: a dark world cut into pale stock.

**Don't borrow:** the red-to-black sky gradient, or the Instagram furniture. And
note the edge treatment — the reference lets its photographic ring meet the pale
ground at a hard line. The brand's rule says feather and vignette instead, so
the world dissolves into the ground rather than being pasted onto it.
