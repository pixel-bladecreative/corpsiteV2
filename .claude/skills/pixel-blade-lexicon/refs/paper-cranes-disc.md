# ref · paper-cranes-disc
**Tags:** `struck`, `dark elegance`

Two cranes in layered cut white paper, wings built from individually cut
feather strips, flying across a flat saturated red disc on near-black. Real
shadows fall from the birds onto the disc and from the feather strips onto the
wings beneath them.

**The mechanic.** The highest-contrast version of the paper-cut idea: near-black
ground, one saturated disc, white subject. Three values total. It is loud and
it works because it commits.

**Borrow:** the value structure — dark ground, one disc, one light subject —
and the shadows *within* the subject, not only beneath it.

**Don't borrow:** the disc at that size. It runs well past the chroma ceiling.
`check-plate.py` failed the first attempt at 35.4%; r≈0.60 of the frame brings
it to 20.7% and passes. Also don't borrow the feather strips — feathers are a
bird, and the mark is a folded object.
