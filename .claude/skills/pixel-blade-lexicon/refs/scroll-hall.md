# ref: scroll-hall

**Tags:** `surreal`, `scale`
**Source:** supplied by Spencer, 2026-08-25. Not stored on disk.

## What is in it

The same device as `lantern-scrolls`, drained of color. Enormous hanging paper
scrolls arranged in a wide curve, each carrying a sparse ink landscape with
large empty margins, some with gold leaf. Warm bone, black, and gold only.

Volumetric light shafts fall between the scrolls through fine haze. Two or three
tiny human figures walk on a pale ramp at the base, each about 3% of frame
height. The floor is seamless and polished, doubling everything.

## What it teaches

**This is the closer reference of the two for Pixel Blade.** It proves the
mechanic does not need saturation — the dislocation survives in a near-
monochrome bone/black/gold palette, which is already almost the brand's own.

It also shows restraint inside the artwork: each scroll carries very little ink
and a great deal of empty paper. That is the same reduction principle the mark
is built on, applied to the environment.

## Caution

The curved, evenly spaced arrangement edges toward a stage set. Pixel Blade
should keep the arrangement irregular so it reads as found rather than staged.
