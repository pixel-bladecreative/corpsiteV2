4. **Orange architectural drawing.** White paper ground; a tower built from

   accents; black hairlines running well past the structure and fading.
   **The strongest statement of lines that continue too far.**

## What they teach

The whole `tech` term, and the day mode. All four sit on a light paper ground,
which is what makes a light page possible at all. Images 3 and 4 supply the
overshooting drafting lines; image 2 supplies the handmade paper-and-board
texture; image 1 supplies the depth-scattered tiles.

Each carries one warm accent and one cool against neutral — orange/charcoal,
ochre/grey, teal/red, rust/grey. That maps onto oxide and petrol.

## Caution

- **Hexagons again** in image 1. Do not transfer; our geometry is folds and
  ninety-degree slabs.
- **Printed text fragments** in images 2 and 4 are decoration there. In this
  brand type is load-bearing.
- Image 1's orange is far hotter than oxide. It belongs to the image gamut, not
  to interface.
