# ref · paper-strata
**Tags:** `struck`, `scale`, `dreamed garden`

A landscape built entirely from cut paper sheets on white. A flat red disc at
the back. Cloud shapes as stacked white cut-outs with visible drop shadows. A
black tree, then range after range of grey mountains, each one a separate sheet
throwing a short soft shadow onto the sheet behind it. The whole thing floats
on a near-white ground with no horizon line.

**The mechanic.** Depth is entirely shadow between planes. No perspective, no
gradient, no atmosphere — just N sheets at N offsets under one consistent
light. The furthest sheets are lighter not because of haze but because they are
cut from lighter stock.

**Also.** The red disc sits at the very back and is *partially covered* by the
subject. It never floats free.

**Borrow:** the whole construction. This is `papercut` mode.

**Don't borrow:** the white ground as a default. In night mode the sheets go
white on sumi; in day mode they go petrol on paper. Graphite sheets on white
went muddy — a mid-value sheet on a light ground has nothing to throw its
shadow against.
