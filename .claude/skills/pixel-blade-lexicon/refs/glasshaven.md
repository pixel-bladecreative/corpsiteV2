# ref: glasshaven

**Tags:** `dark-elegance` (partial — **structure only**)
**Source:** supplied 2026-08-25. Not stored on disk.

Real-estate concept. Each section is a large rounded-corner near-black card
floating on a slate blue-grey ground. Ultra-bold wide display type in white.
Hero image inside the card with nav and overlaid type. A four-up service grid
with images and large outlined numerals. A data table with hairline dividers and
right-aligned figures.

**Teaches:** `numbered-grid`, `data-rows`, and a clear section rhythm.

**Caution — the most off-brand reference in the set.** Rounded floating cards
contradict "hairlines over boxes"; the slate ground contradicts the flat black;
the ultra-bold wide display face contradicts light-weight type and gold-as-ink.
Take the structural rhythm and the data table. Leave the surface entirely.
